Current Research

Internet-Facing Cyber Attack Surface Analysis

My current Master's research focuses on mapping and analysing Internet-facing attack surfaces using passive OSINT techniques.

The research involves asset and service discovery, software identification, CPE mapping, vulnerability analysis using CVE and CVSS, and exploitation likelihood assessment using EPSS.

The objective is to better understand exposed infrastructure, service diversity and vulnerability risk at scale.
