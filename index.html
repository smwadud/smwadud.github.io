Current Research

Mapping Attack Surface of Bangladesh Public Infrastructure

Master's dissertation · University of Waikato · Supervisor: Dr. Vimal Kumar · Expected completion: January 2027

My Master's research maps and analyses the Internet-facing attack surface of Bangladesh's public infrastructure using predominantly passive public-source discovery, controlled DNS validation and Censys service observations. The scope includes government domains, Bangladesh Bank-listed financial institutions and international or foreign-parent sites.

The research involves asset and service discovery, software identification, CPE mapping, potential vulnerability associations using CVE and CVSS, and descriptive threat context using EPSS and CISA KEV.

Public-source discovery DNS validation / ASN / BGP Censys services & software CPE evidence review NVD CVE / CVSS EPSS / CISA KEV context

The objective is to better understand exposed infrastructure, service diversity and potential vulnerability exposure at scale. Software-to-CVE matches require further validation before a vulnerability can be confirmed. EPSS and CISA KEV add descriptive vulnerability-intelligence context.
